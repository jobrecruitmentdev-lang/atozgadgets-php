1. Custom Engine Architecture vs Shopify / WooCommerce

Why custom PHP MVC + Database gives you 100% ownership over your brand, SEO, and margins.

CJ Dropshipping is designed as a supplier & fulfillment engine, not your website database. Your custom database owns customer records, SEO slugs, custom funnels, and order states, insulating your business from vendor lock-in.

CUSTOMER (USA/Global) ──▶ AtoZGadgets (PHP MVC + MySQL) ──▶ Order Saved (Status: PAID) │ └──▶ Background Job ──▶ CJ API v2.0 (createOrderV2) │ └──▶ CJ Warehouse Ships

2. 2026 CJ API Points-Based Rate Limit Architecture

Protecting your 50,000 base daily points limit from exhaustion.

Recommended Architecture

Scheduled Cache-First Ingestion

Storefront queries local MySQL. Cron synchronizes prices and stock every 12 hours. Zero points wasted on casual visitors.

High Risk

Live API Call On Every Page View

Pings CJ on every product click. Consumes 10 points per click; 5,000 visitors will completely freeze your API access.

8. Webhook & Tracking Automation

Secure HMAC-SHA256 receiver for automated shipment notifications.

CJ pushes status updates to https://atozgadgetz.com/api/webhooks/cj signed with your secret openId.

1. CJ Dispatches Package ──▶ Webhook receives tracking number (e.g. 9400100000000000000000) 2. Backend verifies HMAC-SHA256 signature in < 3s (HTTP 200 OK) 3. Shipment record updated (status: 'in_transit') 4. Automated email/SMS sent to customer with live 17Track / USPS tracking link
